Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information FisherCapital.ca collects when you use this website, why it is collected, how it is used, and the choices you have. FisherCapital is a personal lead-capture brand operated by Raymond. F, Mortgage agent (Level 1), FSRA Lic. #M26000144, with Mortgage Commitment, an office of Centum Financial Services Limited Partnership, FSRA Brokerage Lic. #13054. This website serves Ontario residents and is intended for general information and initial inquiry only.

1.What information is collected

When you fill out the intake form on this website, you may provide the following:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • A general description of your mortgage situation (for example, renewal, refinance, self-employed income, a prior decline, or debt you want to consolidate)
  • Your general location or province
  • Any other details you choose to type into the optional notes field

If you book a call, the booking tool may also collect the information needed to schedule and confirm your appointment, such as your name, email, phone number, and chosen time.

This website may also automatically receive limited technical information that is standard for most websites, such as your browser type and general usage data, through the website host. On the self-employed landing page and the thank-you page, and only if you accept the notice shown there, this website uses the Meta Pixel. See the section on cookies and advertising measurement.

Please do not submit sensitive information through this website. The public intake form is not designed to collect your Social Insurance Number (SIN), banking login credentials, document uploads, full financial records, or mortgage application paperwork. Please do not enter any of these into the form or the notes field. If sensitive documents are needed at a later stage, they are collected only through the approved brokerage application process, not through this website.

2.Why this information is collected

Information is collected so that Raymond. F can understand your situation at a high level, prepare for a useful first conversation, respond to your inquiry, and schedule a call if you choose to book one. The goal at this stage is to learn enough context to have an informed conversation, not to assess or process a formal mortgage application.

3.How your information is used

Your information is used to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and follow up with you
  • Prepare for and schedule a phone or video conversation
  • Organize and track your inquiry internally so that nothing is missed
  • Communicate with you about your inquiry through email, phone, or text, using the contact details and preferences you provided

Your information is not sold. It is not rented. It is not used for unrelated marketing by third parties.

4.Third-party tools that may process your information

To run this website and manage inquiries, a small number of third-party service providers may process the information you submit. Each provider operates under its own privacy and security practices, which are outside the control of FisherCapital. The current tools are:

  • Tally. Receives and stores the answers you type into the intake form, including your name, email, phone number and the details you give about your situation.
  • Calendly. Receives the name, email, phone number and time slot you provide when you book a call.
  • Make.com. Receives your form or lead-form submission in transit and passes it into the lead-tracking system.
  • Zoho CRM. Receives and stores your enquiry record, including your contact details, the details you shared and any notes added during follow-up.
  • Google Workspace. Receives the content of any email you exchange with Raymond. F, and hosts the FisherCapital mailbox.
  • Google Fonts. Receives your IP address and browser information when your browser requests the fonts used on these pages.
  • GitHub Pages. Hosts this website and receives the standard technical request information that any web host receives, such as your IP address and browser type.
  • Vercel, Supabase and Anthropic. Together they run the website chat: Vercel receives and serves the chat requests, Anthropic receives the message text in order to generate a reply, and Supabase stores the conversation. See the section on website chat.
  • Meta Platforms, Inc. and its affiliates. Receives page view and form-completion events from the Meta Pixel if you accept the notice on the two pages where it runs, and receives the information you type into a Facebook or Instagram lead form. See the section on cookies and advertising measurement.

These providers may store data on servers located inside or outside of Canada. When information is stored or processed outside of Canada, it may be subject to the laws of the country where it is held.

5.Cookies, advertising measurement and social media lead forms

What the Meta Pixel does. The Meta Pixel is a small piece of code from Meta Platforms, Inc. It uses cookies and similar identifiers stored by your browser. When it is active it sends events to Meta, such as the fact that a page was viewed and that a form was completed. Meta uses those events to measure how the ads perform and to build audiences of people who have visited these pages, so that ads can be shown to similar people or to you again. Automatic collection of extra information from the page is turned off.

Where it runs and how to decline. The pixel loads only on the self-employed landing page and on the thank-you page, and only after you accept the notice shown at the bottom of those pages. It does not load on the homepage, on the other topic pages, or on this Privacy Policy. If you choose Decline, no pixel is loaded. You can change your choice by clearing this site's stored data in your browser, and you can control cookies generally through your browser settings. You can also review and change how Meta uses your information for ads in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings.

Facebook and Instagram lead forms. If you fill out a lead form inside Facebook or Instagram rather than on this website, the information you submit there, which may include your name, email address, phone number, city, province, business structure, timing and what you are trying to do, is delivered to FisherCapital through Make.com and stored in Zoho CRM. Meta also keeps a copy under its own terms.

How long information is kept. These periods are drafts and are subject to review by the brokerage:

  • Enquiry records are kept for 24 months after the last contact, unless a mortgage application proceeds, in which case they are kept under the brokerage and FSRA record-keeping rules, currently 6 years.
  • Chat transcripts are intended to be kept for 90 days once the scheduled deletion routine is in place. Until then they are kept until deleted on request.
  • Meta pixel data is kept by Meta according to Meta's own retention practices.

Consent and marketing messages. The consent you give when you submit an enquiry covers contact about that enquiry only. It is not consent to receive marketing. If FisherCapital ever wants to send you marketing messages, that requires a separate opt-in from you, and every such message will identify the sender and include a way to unsubscribe.

6.Website chat

Some pages on this website offer a chat window. It is a convenience tool for general questions, not a secure channel and not a place to apply for a mortgage.

Please do not enter a Social Insurance Number, account numbers, identification numbers, or documents into the chat. If sensitive information is needed later, it is collected only through the brokerage's approved application process.

What happens to a chat message: the message is sent to the FisherCapital chat service hosted on Vercel. It is processed by Anthropic's Claude model in order to generate a reply. The message and the reply are stored in a Supabase database. Raymond. F can read the stored conversations. Replies are generated by software and are general information only, not mortgage advice.

There is no automatic deletion schedule for chat transcripts yet, so messages are kept until they are deleted on request. A 90 day deletion routine is planned. To request deletion of a chat conversation, email Raymond@fishercapital.ca and describe roughly when the chat took place, so the conversation can be found and removed.

7.When information may be shared with the brokerage

FisherCapital is a personal brand operated through Mortgage Commitment, an office of Centum Financial Services Limited Partnership, FSRA Brokerage Lic. #13054. If, after speaking with Raymond. F, you choose to move forward with a mortgage inquiry or application, your information may be shared with the brokerage and used within the brokerage's approved application process. This happens only when you decide to proceed. Sharing with lenders, insurers, or other parties involved in a mortgage transaction is handled through that approved brokerage process and is governed by the disclosures and consents you receive at that stage.

8.This website does not provide mortgage advice or lending decisions

The content on this website is general information only. It does not constitute mortgage advice, a recommendation, an offer to lend, a pre-approval, or a lending decision. Submitting the intake form does not create a client relationship and does not guarantee any outcome. Any mortgage advice or decision happens only through a proper review within the approved brokerage process and is subject to lender approval.

9.What you should not submit through this website

To protect your privacy, please do not submit any of the following through the public intake form, the notes field, the chat window, or general email:

  • Your Social Insurance Number (SIN)
  • Banking login credentials or passwords
  • Uploaded documents or scans
  • Full financial records or statements
  • Completed mortgage application paperwork

If any of this information is needed later, it is requested and collected only through the brokerage's secure and approved application process.

10.Access, correction, and deletion

You may ask what personal information has been collected about you, ask for corrections if something is inaccurate, or ask for your information to be deleted, where applicable. Some information may need to be retained to meet legal, regulatory, or record-keeping obligations that apply to licensed mortgage professionals in Ontario. To make a request, use the contact details below. A reasonable effort will be made to respond within a reasonable timeframe.

11.Data security

Reasonable care is taken to limit the information collected through this website and to work with established service providers. However, no website or method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be fully secure. You share information through this website at your own discretion, and you are encouraged to avoid sending sensitive information through the public form or the chat window, as described above.

12.Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time, for example if tools change or new tools are added. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent version. Continued use of the website after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
