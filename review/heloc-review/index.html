Know your options

HELOC / home renovations, explained simply.

A home equity line of credit, or HELOC, lets you borrow up to an approved limit, repay it and use the credit again. It can help fund a renovation in stages. Raymond can explain the payment rules, costs and a plan to pay down the balance.

Use funds in stages

Draw within an approved limit as you need funds. Lender conditions and access rules apply.

Plan beyond the interest

Compare the alternatives

A lump-sum home equity loan or refinancing may fit differently. Review setup costs and the existing mortgage too.

Your home secures a HELOC. Rates may change. Interest-only payments do not reduce the amount borrowed. Missed payments can put your home at risk.

Questions you might have.

Do I need income and credit to qualify?

A HELOC still requires lender review. Equity alone is not an approval. The lender assesses your circumstances, property and ability to repay.

Can the payments change?

Yes. Many HELOCs have variable rates. A rate increase can raise interest costs and required payments.

Is a HELOC a home equity loan?

Both use your home as security, but they work differently. A HELOC is reusable credit within a limit. A home equity loan usually advances one lump sum.

What happens after I contact Ray?

Ray reviews your situation and explains the next steps. If documents are needed later, he will explain how to provide them securely. This first step is not an approval.
