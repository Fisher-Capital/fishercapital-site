THE APPROPRIATE LENDING ROUTE

One review. More than one possible route.

Self-employed income can be documented through business profits, salary, dividends or contracts. Different lenders assess the evidence differently.

The review considers the property, income, existing obligations, financing goal and costs. A bank, alternative lender or private lender may be appropriate. There may also be no suitable borrowing option. Home equity alone does not guarantee approval.

PRIVATE MORTGAGE OPTIONS

Understand the costs.
Plan how it will be repaid.

A private mortgage may be an option when standard bank financing does not fit. Private mortgages can have higher costs and shorter terms.

Before proceeding, review the interest, applicable lender and brokerage fees, legal and appraisal costs, payment obligations, term, and renewal or prepayment conditions. A realistic repayment plan matters. Refinancing with a bank later is not guaranteed, and missed obligations can put the property at risk.

Private financing is assessed for suitability alongside other appropriate options. It is not the automatic result of a self-employed enquiry.

Questions before getting started

Does being self-employed mean I need a private mortgage?

No. Self-employed borrowers may fit bank or alternative-lender programs, depending on income documentation and the full application. Private financing is considered only where appropriate.

Can I access equity without replacing my existing mortgage?

Some options may involve additional financing rather than replacing the first mortgage. Availability, costs and suitability depend on existing mortgage terms, the property, obligations and lender criteria. An initial review does not promise a specific product.

What should I understand about a private mortgage?

Review the total cost, payment obligations, length of the term, conditions for renewal or early repayment, and the plan to repay. An expected refinance or sale can be delayed or unavailable. Approval of a future mortgage is not guaranteed.

Will the initial conversation affect my credit?

No credit check is part of the initial conversation. If a formal application follows, the required authorization and process will be explained first.

Should I send documents now?

No. Do not send a SIN, account information, tax returns or identity documents through the initial enquiry or by text. Any later documents should use the brokerage's approved secure process.
