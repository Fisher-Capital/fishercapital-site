Questions you might have.

Does owning a home mean I qualify?

No. Available equity is only part of the review. Property, income, credit, other debts and lender rules also matter.

Is this the same as a HELOC?

No. A home equity loan usually gives you one lump sum. A HELOC is a revolving credit limit you can draw on, repay and use again within its terms.

Can I leave my existing mortgage in place?

Sometimes. We compare any additional secured loan with refinancing, including the existing lender’s conditions, penalties and the full cost.
