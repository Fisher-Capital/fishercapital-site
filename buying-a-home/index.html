Who This Page Is For

Does any of this sound like your purchase?

You don't need all of these to be worth a conversation. One is enough.

  • You're self-employed, incorporated, or paid on commission, and your tax returns don't show what the business actually earns.
  • You already applied somewhere and were declined, and nobody explained which part of the file caused it.
  • Your credit history has an event on it, or you are still rebuilding after one.
  • Part of the down payment is a gift, coming from a sale, or arriving from outside the country.
  • You're new to Canada, or new to the workforce, and you don't yet have the history a standard process expects.
  • You're buying with someone whose situation is different from yours, and you're not sure how the two get read together.
What a Review Covers

What I actually look at before saying anything.

A purchase file gets read in roughly this order. None of it needs documents from you on day one.

01

How the income is earned

Employed, self-employed, incorporated, commissioned, or a mix. How you're paid changes which documentation approaches may be worth pursuing, and which lenders may be able to consider the file.

02

The down payment

How much, where it comes from, and how long it has been where it is. Source of funds is something every lender asks about, so it is better understood early than late.

03

Credit and existing obligations

What is on the bureau, what is still being carried, and how those get read alongside the new payment. No credit pull happens for this first conversation.

04

The property and the timeline

What you're buying, where, and when you need to close. Timelines are set by the lender and the file, and a realistic one becomes clearer once the full picture is understood.

Whether any lender would approve a purchase, and on what terms, depends on that lender's program and a full application. Nothing on this page is a guarantee of approval, timeline, rate or amount.
