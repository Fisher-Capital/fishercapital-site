What I Can Look At With You

Four paths that come up most often for self-employed borrowers

Bank Statement Qualifying
Some lenders may consider 12 months of business deposits alongside other documentation.
2-Year Income Averaging
Some lenders may consider income averaged over two years, depending on the program and the full application. A lower year does not automatically end an application; treatment depends on the lender and the overall picture.
Stated Income with Support
Some lenders may consider stated income backed by contracts, invoices, or business financials, without depending exclusively on CRA-reported net income. Documentation requirements vary by lender and loan amount.
Regulated alternative lenders
Alternative lenders that are regulated financial institutions offer mortgage products for income profiles that do not fit a standard bank program. Pricing and terms differ from a chartered bank and are set by each lender.
