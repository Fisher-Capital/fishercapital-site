Questions you might have.

Does this mean no tax documents?

No blanket promise applies. Depending on the lender and program, tax documents and other records may still be needed. We explain the requirements for the options being considered.

Can gross business revenue qualify me?

Gross revenue is not the same as income available to pay a mortgage. Lenders may assess expenses, ownership and other factors. Deposits do not automatically become personal qualifying income.
