Information collected

The Alberta enquiry form requests your name, email, phone, whether you own or co-own property in Alberta, financing goal, work type and timing. It also produces submission and attribution records such as Meta lead, form, campaign and ad identifiers, submission time and the contact-permission wording used. Follow-up may add contact attempts, appointment details and enquiry notes.

The form is not a mortgage application. Do not submit a SIN, account or login details, financial documents, identity documents, property values, mortgage balances or detailed income information through it or ordinary messages. A later application uses the brokerage-approved secure process with its own disclosures and permissions.

Purposes and contact choices

We use the information to respond to the enquiry, arrange an initial conversation, understand the requested service at a general level, maintain enquiry records and assess campaign results. The initial form does not make an automated credit, approval or product-selection decision.

Submitting requests contact by phone, email or text about this enquiry. It does not enrol you in unrelated marketing. You can ask to stop follow-up by replying STOP to a text or contacting Raymond@fishercapital.ca / (416) 898-0181. Withdrawing permission may limit our ability to continue the enquiry; records that must be retained can remain subject to legal obligations.

Providers and disclosures

Meta receives your native lead-form submission and processes information under its own terms. The proposed Alberta intake sends submitted details through Make to Zoho CRM for Raymond and authorized brokerage personnel to manage the enquiry. Calendly receives the details you provide when booking. Email correspondence is handled through Google Workspace. The website host receives technical request information such as IP address and browser details.

We remain responsible for our handling of your information when using service providers. Access is limited to the relevant work. Information is not sold or used by us to enrol you in unrelated marketing. If you proceed with an application, sharing with lenders, insurers or other transaction participants is explained through the approved brokerage process.

Retention and safeguards

Enquiry information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for the stated purpose and applicable business, legal and regulatory obligations, then securely deleted or anonymized under the approved retention schedule. If an enquiry becomes a mortgage application, the brokerage record-retention requirements apply. Service providers may maintain their own records and backups under their terms.

Reasonable safeguards include limiting access to authorized personnel and using the brokerage-approved process for sensitive documents. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Avoid sending financial documents by text, ordinary email, social-media message or booking notes.
