Know your options

Debt consolidation, explained simply.

A refinance or home equity loan may let you combine existing debts into secured borrowing. That can change the monthly payments, but a smaller payment is not always a cheaper loan. Raymond can help compare the fees, repayment time and total cost.

Plan for the balance

Consolidation moves debt; it does not erase it. A repayment plan and future spending both matter.

Moving unsecured debts into borrowing against your home puts the home at risk if payments are missed. A lower monthly payment can mean more interest over a longer repayment period. Savings are not guaranteed.

Questions you might have.

Does debt consolidation erase my debt?

No. It replaces or combines debts with new borrowing. You still owe the balance and must repay it under the new terms.

Will my monthly payment be lower?

It may change, but a reduction is not guaranteed. Even a lower payment can cost more overall if you repay for longer or incur additional fees.

What is the main risk of using home equity?

Debts that were unsecured can become secured by your home. If you miss payments, the home can be at risk. Compare other options as well.
