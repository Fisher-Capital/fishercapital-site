Why a Mortgage Agent?

It's a reasonable question. Here's the straightforward answer.

01

Banks offer their own products. A mortgage agent has access to multiple lenders across different lending categories, which matters when your situation doesn't fit a standard profile.

02

Banks underwrite to standard criteria. A file assessed only on tax-reported income may not fit every lender's program.

03

Banks don't typically explain what didn't work, or what would. A mortgage agent's job is to understand your full picture and outline which documentation approaches may be worth pursuing. Not just run the application and deliver a decision.

04

In most residential mortgage transactions, the mortgage agent is compensated by the lender when the mortgage closes, not by you. In some situations involving alternative lenders, a fee may apply. If that is the case for your file, it will be explained and agreed to in writing before anything moves forward. Private mortgages are outside the scope of a Level 1 mortgage agent and are not arranged here, so no fee for one is ever quoted on this site. A file that appears to fit only a private lender is referred to a licensed mortgage broker at Centum Financial Services Limited Partnership.
